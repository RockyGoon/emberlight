Emberlight is a neon top-down arena survival shooter: you are the last ember, a small wisp of light, holding back waves of shadow wisps. Shoot, collect light shards, and choose an upgrade between waves to survive the dark — across two realms, against three bosses, collecting relics from the fallen.

## Depth

- **3 bosses, one every 5 waves:** the Void Wisp (wave 5, radial orb bursts + minion summons), the Tidekeeper (wave 10, a rotating spiral of orbs + a telegraphed dash straight at you), the Ashen Sovereign (wave 15, a full ring of orbs + tank summons, enraging below half health) — then the cycle repeats, harder.
- **2 realms, alternating every 5 waves:** the Ashen Expanse (waves 1–5) and the Sunken Deep (waves 6–10, teal drowned arena, faster spawns, more shooters), then back again.
- **14 upgrades** (8 original + 6 new): Orbiting Ember (flames circle you, burning what they touch), Ember Nova (a shockwave every 6 s), Kindled Edge (+20% crit chance, double damage), Slow Burn (regenerate a heart), Swift Bolt (bolts 25% faster and farther), Gilded Shards (shards worth +5).
- **6 relics, dropped in pairs by bosses:** Phoenix Feather (revive once at 1 heart) + Wisp Lantern (magnet doubled) from the Void Wisp; Storm Sigil (kills chain-zap a nearby shadow) + Frostbrand Aura (nearby shadows slowed 30%) from the Tidekeeper; Bloodmoon Charm (every 15th shard heals 1 heart) + Titan Anchor (bolts 50% wider) from the Sovereign. Each one changes a real rule for the rest of the run.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.
